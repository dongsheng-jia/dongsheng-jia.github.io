贾东升Dongsheng Jia

I am a PhD student in Finance at Shanghai University of Finance and Economics. My advisor is Ling Feng, and my co-advisor is Xiaohan Wang.我是上海财经大学金融学院的金融学博士生，导师为冯玲教授，副导师为 Xiaohan Wang 老师。

My current research focuses on financial linkages between firms, such as trade credit, and how they shape firm behavior and macroeconomic outcomes.我目前的研究关注企业间的金融链条（例如商业信贷）如何影响企业行为，以及由此产生的宏观经济后果。

My approach combines empirical research with macroeconomic modeling, including DSGE models and numerical solution methods such as value function iteration (VFI).在方法上，我结合经验研究与宏观建模，使用 DSGE 模型以及价值函数迭代（VFI）等数值求解方法。

On my Idea? page, I share a few ideas (quite possibly wrong) and economic “facts” (quite possibly another case of ice cream sales and shark attacks). Discussion and collaboration are welcome.我也在 Idea? 记录一些个人想法（也许是错的）和有趣的经济“事实”（也许又是冰淇淋销量与鲨鱼袭击的故事）。欢迎交流与合作。
