Dongsheng Jia 贾东升

贾东升Dongsheng Jia

I am a PhD student in Finance at Shanghai University of Finance and Economics, advised by Professor Ling Feng.我是上海财经大学金融学院的金融学博士生，导师为冯玲教授。

My research interests lie in macroeconomics, macro-finance, and international economics. I study the connections between trade credit, bank lending, and supply chains.我的研究兴趣包括宏观经济学、宏观金融与国际经济学，主要关注商业信贷、银行融资与供应链之间的联系。
